Hybrid Work Policy
Northwind Travel Ltd. Version 2.1, valid from 1 January.

1. Purpose
This policy explains when staff may work from home and what we expect from everyone, at home and in the office. Our aim is simple: do good work, stay reachable, and keep company and customer data safe wherever you are.

2. Who it covers
The policy covers all permanent and fixed-term employees after their first three months. Staff in customer-facing roles at our travel desks agree a schedule with their team lead, because a desk must always be staffed during opening hours.

3. Office days and home days
Each team has two anchor days a week when everyone comes to the office. Your team lead chooses the anchor days and shares them at least two weeks before they change. On the other three days you may work from home or from the office. Working from abroad is not allowed for more than 10 working days a year, and every trip needs written approval from HR in advance, for tax and insurance reasons.

4. Core hours and being reachable
Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 on every working day. During core hours you must be reachable by chat and phone and answer within one hour. Outside core hours you may plan your own time, as long as you work your contracted hours. Show your status honestly in the calendar, including breaks longer than 30 minutes.

5. Equipment and costs
The company provides a laptop, a headset and, on request, one external monitor for home use. Staff who work from home at least two days a week receive a home-office allowance of 30 euros a month with their salary. Internet and electricity at home are covered by this allowance and are not paid separately.

6. Security
Only use company devices for company work. Lock your screen whenever you step away. Never work on customer data in public places where others can see your screen, such as cafes or trains. Use the company VPN for every connection outside the office. Report a lost or stolen device to IT within two hours.

7. When things do not work
If hybrid work is not working for you or your team, talk to your team lead first. The team lead may ask a person to spend more days in the office for a period of up to three months, for example during training or after repeated missed deadlines. This must be explained in writing.

8. Review
HR reviews this policy every year with the staff council. Questions go to hr@northwind.example.
